## Cybersecurity — practitioner & institutional

### James Kettle — jameskettle.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Research grouped *thematically by attack class* (HTTP Request Smuggling, Web Cache Poisoning, Tools & automation) rather than chronologically. No cards, no screenshots, no tech badges. A "mentors and inspirations" section. Publishes methodology, not just results.
**Principle:** Thematic grouping converts a list of outputs into a demonstrated domain — a structural argument available immediately, without seniority.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — strongly.** The single most important navigational idea found. Maps directly onto the roadmap's 12 tracks.
**Caveat carried forward:** his austerity is affordable because his name carries credibility. Het cannot borrow it wholesale.

### Trail of Bits — blog.trailofbits.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Depth signalled by naming precise techniques in titles. 1–2 sentence abstracts. Individual researcher attribution inside an institutional brand. Austere, minimal imagery.
**Principle:** Linguistic precision *is* a credibility mechanism. Vague improvement-language signals nothing.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes.** Sets the vocabulary standard for every heading and abstract on the site.

### PortSwigger Research — portswigger.net/research `[T]`
**Learned:** Three parallel axes into one corpus — vulnerability class, researcher, date. An annual "Top 10 Hacking Techniques" as a recurring temporal anchor.
**Principle:** A serious research body needs more than one way in; a recurring dated artefact creates scheduled return visits.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes.** Multi-axis IA (domain × state × chronology).

### Sam Curry — samcurry.net `[T]`
**Learned:** Writeups as investigation narratives. Cards whose excerpts state the actual finding. Only date and read-time as metadata. Consistent co-discoverer credit.
**Principle:** A card is legitimate when it carries a claim. Security work is inherently narrative.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — supports narrative case studies over summary cards.

### Daniel Miessler — danielmiessler.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Leads with a thesis, not a job title. Named projects (TELOS, Human 3.0, PAI) with their own identities and homes. Archive to 1999.
**Principle:** A point of view is more memorable and more defensible than a skill list.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes.** Directly serves the brief's demand that visitors think "this person has a point of view."

### tl;dr sec — tldrsec.com `[T]`
**Learned:** 340+ issues of sustained cadence as the credibility mechanism. An explicit time contract with the reader ("7 min/week"). Content typed by *format*.
**Principle:** Consistency over time is credibility available to the early-career. Typing by format is weaker than typing by epistemic status.
**Influenced direction?** **Partly.** Cadence: yes. Format-typing: rejected in favour of Appleton's model.

### rez0 / Joseph Thacker — rez0.com `[T]`
**Learned:** tmux-style bracketed footer `[0:main] [1:security] [2:ai]`. Terminal prompt as identity device.
**Principle:** Layered signalling — a detail neutral to a recruiter and precise to a practitioner serves both at once.
**Influenced direction?** **Narrowly.** Establishes the *only* condition under which technical iconography survives the §26 ban. The device itself is too specific to reuse.

## Cybersecurity — the saturated archetype (anti-references)

### Adharv K T — adharvkt.github.io/portfolio `[R]`
**Learned (measured):** neon green `rgb(44,214,38)` on black; Poppins on 164/174 elements; ALL-CAPS headings; "COURSES COMPLETED" / "LICENSES ACCQUIRED"; percentage skill bars (SOC 99%); four tool-exercise "projects"; phrases "dedicated cybersecurity professional", "proven track record".
**Principle:** Credential accumulation is not capability; unfalsifiable numbers read as decoration.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes, negatively.** The genericity baseline the final design must beat.

### Zyekh Abdul — zyekh.cloud `[R]`
**Learned (measured):** JetBrains Mono as the entire body face; `rgb(18,19,26)`; `SYSTEM_STATUS: ONLINE`; `SYSTEM_OVERVIEW` / `CORE_REPOSITORIES` / `TECHNICAL_DOMAIN`; "INITIALIZE CONTACT"; terminal styling with no terminal function.
**Principle:** A metaphor applied only to vocabulary is costume; applied to structure, it is architecture.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes, negatively** — and as a specific warning for any concept with a system metaphor.

### Raif Kaya — raifkaya.nl `[T]`
**Learned:** Five *tools* listed under "Portfolio"; percentage bars with year counts; "Master skills that are beyond legal"; About→Portfolio→Services→Skills→Experience→Contact.
**Principle:** Tools-as-portfolio is the category's defining structural error. Outlaw framing destroys credibility with GRC buyers.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes, negatively.** Especially pointed: Het's toolset is the *same list*.

### GitHub topic `cybersecurity-portfolio` — 20 repositories `[L]`
**Learned:** "modern, responsive portfolio website" recurring verbatim; "Glassmorphic terminal UI with matrix backdrop"; SOC/SIEM labs dominant; GRC represented once in twenty.
**Principle:** The saturated crowd competes on SOC labs. GRC/audit ground is comparatively empty.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — strategically decisive.** Basis for the positioning hypothesis.

## Exceptional digital experience

### Dennis Snellenberg — dennissnellenberg.com `[R]`
**Learned (measured):** `canvasCount = 0`. Custom "Dennis Sans". H1 192px, constant weight 450, hierarchy by size alone. Charcoal on 376 elements, blue accent on 8.
**Principle:** The most-awarded portfolio inspected uses *fewer* effects than the student security sites. Accent scarcity (~2% of elements) is what makes an accent read as intentional.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — strongly.** The empirical counter-argument to "memorable = animated". Justifies spending on typography and restraint rather than a 3D scene.

### Bruno Simon — bruno-simon.com `[L]`
**Learned:** A drivable Three.js world where navigation is the game. Multiple major awards.
**Principle:** A spectacular interaction is justified only when it demonstrates the exact skill being sold. Bruno Simon teaches Three.js, so the showpiece is *evidence*.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes, as a boundary.** Het is not selling WebGL; the same mechanic would be decoration and would undermine him. Defines the justification test for any ambitious interaction.

### Awwwards portfolio category — 31 featured sites `[L]`
**Learned:** The arena is dominated by design, film and studio portfolios. Two of 31 use an OS metaphor in their name (ZUI_OS, PouyaOS).
**Principle:** Security engineering is essentially absent from this arena — the arbitrage opportunity. Separately, the "personal OS" metaphor is in active circulation and is not untrodden ground.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — establishes the craft bar, and flags one concept's originality risk.

## Editorial, research & information design

### Bartosz Ciechanowski — ciechanow.ski `[T]`
**Learned:** Draggable simulations embedded *inside* the prose at the point of confusion. Progressive complexity. Reader-driven motion, never autoplay. Explicitly not a card grid.
**Principle:** Interaction that serves comprehension is never gimmicky, because removing it costs understanding.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — the highest-value cross-domain transfer.** Security concepts (attack paths, entitlement chains, trust boundaries) are exactly the relational systems that static text explains badly.

### Maggie Appleton — maggieappleton.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Content typed by *epistemic status* — Essays ("opinionated, with an agenda") vs Notes ("things I don't entirely understand yet") vs Patterns. Visible ageing ("over 3 years ago"). A "Now" page in primary nav.
**Principle:** Publicly labelling uncertainty *increases* credibility. Make epistemic status a navigational axis, not a badge.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — structurally decisive.** The proven solution to brief §6 (BUILT / IN PROGRESS / RESEARCH / FUTURE must never blur).
**Copying risk flagged: MEDIUM-HIGH.** "Digital garden", "Garden/Notes/Patterns" and plant metaphors are strongly owned by Appleton. Adopt the structure; never the vocabulary.

### Distill — distill.pub `[T]`
**Learned:** Articles labelled Peer-reviewed / Commentary / Editorial / Thread, with public linked review threads. Mission framed around "research debt".
**Principle:** Making your verification process visible beats asserting rigour.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — supports a "how I verified this" convention.

### The Pudding — pudding.cool `[T]`
**Learned:** Essays numbered sequentially (#224…#198). An "Updating" filter marking living pieces. Question-form subtitles.
**Principle:** Numbering makes accumulation legible; an explicit "still changing" state is honest *and* a return hook.
**Influenced direction?** **Partly.** The "Updating" state: yes. Sequential numbering: deferred until there is a body of work.

### Josh W. Comeau — joshwcomeau.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Explorable examples inside articles; a curated "Popular Content" list; warm voice with deep material.
**Principle:** Voice is a durable identity asset and cannot be templated. Curated entry points prevent visitors landing on the weakest work.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes.**

### nan.fyi — nan.fyi `[T]`
**Learned:** "Rebuild X to understand it" — a debugger, Babel, SVG paths.
**Principle:** The reconstruction format lets an early-career engineer publish real value without needing novel findings — and involves no client data, sidestepping confidentiality entirely.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — a realistic content strategy for Het's actual stage.

### Observable — observablehq.com `[T]`
**Learned:** A minimap giving an overview of the reactive dataflow graph.
**Principle:** If you show a complex structure, give the visitor a way to see the whole before exploring a part.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes, conditionally** — mandatory mitigation for any graph-based concept.

### Linear — linear.app `[T]`
**Learned:** Capability staged numerically (1.0 Intake, 2.0 Plan, 3.0 Build).
**Principle:** Sequencing complexity beats presenting it at once.
**Influenced direction?** **Marginally.** *Honesty note:* the fetch returned navigation markup rather than design substance. Linear's widely-discussed visual system could **not** be verified here and no claims about it are made.

# ADDED 2026-08-14 — Phase 1 continuation

**Method note:** the browser runtime was unavailable in this session, so every entry below is `[T]` or `[L]`. **No new reference carries measured colour or typography values.**

## Confidential work presentation *(Gap 1 — new category)*

### Bishop Fox — bishopfox.com/customers `[T]`
**Learned:** Problem → approach → outcome. Mixed disclosure — major brands named (Google, Amazon, LastPass), others anonymised ("a Fortune 500 utility"). Service categories named; vulnerabilities never disclosed.
**Principle:** Permission-based naming where available, descriptor labels where not — with no explicit confidentiality disclaimers.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — supplies the template for Het's CSCRF/IRDAI presentation.

### Doyensec — phishing case study `[T]`
**Learned:** Client anonymised ("a Communication Platform as a Service") alongside *substantial* technical depth — specific Unicode codepoints, regex flaws, browser differences. Confidentiality achieved by **strategic omission, not disclaimers**.
**Principle:** Anonymity does not require vagueness. Hide the client; show the engineering.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — decisively.** The single best model found, and the evidence that **disconfirmed** the "advertise the withholding" idea.

### Include Security — includesecurity.com `[T]`
**Learned:** **No clients named and no case studies at all.** Credibility from methodology, team credentials, breadth. "Standard practice in security consulting where discretion itself signals trustworthiness."
**Principle:** Confidentiality needs no narrative framing.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes, negatively** — strongly disconfirms O2's novelty.

### Pentest reporting & anonymised case-study conventions `[L]`
**Learned:** Codified norms — redacted previews, `[REDACTED]` markers, methodology as the credibility signal (test type, access level, window, OWASP WSTG / PTES). Descriptor labels ("a Series B fintech") are standard.
**Principle:** Presenting confidential work is a **solved, documented** problem.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — demoted O2 to table stakes.

## Cybersecurity *(Gap 5 expansion)*

### Ranveer Kohli — bugatsec.dev `[T]`
**Learned:** Companies → Findings → Skills → Projects → Contact. CVSS/severity labels, logo wall, category badges. **No hacker motifs whatsoever.** Characterised as *"intentional genericization through absence of personality markers."*
**Principle:** The saturation threat has moved from cyberpunk to **corporate sameness**.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — the most important cautionary reference in the corpus.** It is precisely what The Register becomes without a voice layer.

### Nikki Sadvand — nikki-65.github.io `[T]`
**Learned:** Junior SOC Analyst framing, badge skills, MITRE/NIST references, direct links to PDF and repo artefacts. Again no hacker aesthetic.
**Principle:** Linking to a real artefact beats describing a capability.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — second confirmation of professionalisation; artefact-linking is directly adoptable.

### GitHub topic `security-portfolio` — 20 repositories `[L]`
**Learned:** More GRC presence than the first tag. Sampling caveat: one user accounts for 6 of 20.
**Principle:** GRC is under-represented (~12–15%), **not near-absent**.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — forced a more modest positioning claim.

**Principle:** **Structured records and personality are not in tension.**
**Influenced direction?** **Yes — decisively.** This is the evidence that rescues The Register from the bugatsec failure mode.

### Jesper Landberg — jesperlandberg.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Awards-led credibility; explicit role transparency ("lead or sole developer").
**Principle:** Stating your exact contribution on shared work is a cheap, high-integrity signal.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — became opportunity O16, unusually relevant to team-based audit work.

### Renaud Rohlinger — renaudrohlinger.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Preloader gate; bilingual framing; animated cat during loading. **Most of the site NOT VERIFIED** — JS-rendered, and no browser runtime available.

## Information visualisation *(Gap 3 expansion)*

### R2D3 — Visual Introduction to Machine Learning `[T]`
**Learned:** Scroll-driven progressive disclosure; concrete example (SF vs NY homes); tradeoffs shown visually; an explicit **"Reality check"** section.
**Principle:** Scrollytelling teaches relational systems well and is far cheaper than manipulable simulation.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — raised Control Surface's feasibility from 3 to 5 and supplied a realistic flagship format.

### Our World in Data — ourworldindata.org `[T]`
**Learned:** Layered entry (Popular pages + systematic taxonomy); source labelled on every chart; data limitations stated as standard editorial practice.
**Principle:** Layered entry beats audience segmentation.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — confirms the layering strategy; fourth independent instance supporting O8.

### Setosa — Explained Visually `[T]`
**Learned:** 10 numbered episodes, one concept each, playful taglines.
**Principle:** Small, numbered, finishable pieces are a sustainable format.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — revised the earlier "defer numbering" position.

### Data Sketches — datasketch.es `[T]`
**Learned:** 12 themes × 2 creators; each project pairs the artefact with a technical process write-up; deliberately shows "there is never just one right answer."
**Principle:** Process is publishable before results are.
**Influenced direction?** **Yes** — became O13, directly solving what Het can publish now.

### Visual Cinnamon — visualcinnamon.com `[T]`
**Learned:** Three-way split (DataViz commercial / DataArt personal / CHART book); client logos; method communicated through published books.
**Principle:** Separating commercial from personal work lets each be judged on its own terms.
**Influenced direction?** **Marginally.**
