# Homepage Information Architecture

**Date:** 2026-08-14 (Phase 3) · Information sequence only. **No visual design.**

---

## 1. Challenging the proposed sequence

Phase 3 proposed: IDENTITY → CREDIBILITY → CURIOSITY → DEPTH → CONNECTION.

**One change recommended: CURIOSITY and CREDIBILITY should swap, because for Het they are the same act.**

The conventional sequence assumes credibility is established by *credentials*, then curiosity is provoked by *content*. But Het's credentials are ordinary — a recent degree, an internship, certificates. If the homepage leads with them it spends its strongest position on its weakest evidence.

His credibility comes from the same place his interest does: **the work is real and the thinking is visible.** One well-chosen investigation excerpt establishes both simultaneously.

**Revised sequence:**

> **IDENTITY → EVIDENCE-OF-REAL-WORK → REASONING → DEPTH → CONNECTION**

Where "evidence of real work" and "reasoning" are delivered by the same element, not two.

---

## 2. Information sequence

### Position 1 — Identity *(0–5 seconds)*

**Must convey:** who he is · what he works on · that it is professional work, not coursework.

**Content:** name; a single plain sentence of what he does; nothing else competing.

**Must not:** a tagline · a mission statement · "passionate about…" · animated text · a scroll gate.

**Test:** a recruiter reading only this can categorise him correctly. Nothing below the fold is required for that.

### Position 2 — What he actually does *(5–30 seconds)*

**Must convey:** that the work is real, and what kind it is.

**Content:** two or three sentences establishing testing *and* regulated assurance, and that both are professional. Written in the reasoned first-person voice, not as a bio.

**Must not:** skills · tools · certifications · counters. All are metadata, and all belong on About.

### Position 3 — Reasoning, demonstrated *(30 seconds – 2 minutes)*

**The most important position on the page, and the one most portfolios waste.**

**Must convey:** *this person shows their thinking, including its limits.*

**Content:** the most recent or strongest investigation, given real space — enough of the question and enough of one unresolved element that a visitor understands the shape without clicking.

**Why an excerpt rather than a list:** a list of titles proves nothing about reasoning. A visitor who reads only the homepage should still leave having *seen* the brand behaviour rather than been told about it.

**Must not:** a card grid · screenshots · three equal-weight teasers.

### Position 4 — Depth available *(2–5 minutes)*

**Must convey:** there is more, and it is organised.

**Content:** a short list of further investigations — titles, dates, domain, status. Text. Then recent field notes.

**Must not:** everything. This is an invitation, not the index.

### Position 5 — Connection *(exit)*

**Content:** how to reach him; where the code lives. Plain.

**Must not:** a form (adds abuse surface for no benefit at this scale) · social walls · a newsletter signup promising a cadence that does not exist.

## 3. Time-based understanding

| Time | Understands | Believes | Remembers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5s** | Name, discipline, that the work is professional | — | — |
| **30s** | He tests systems *and* works on regulated assurance | This is real experience, not labs | — |
| **2min** | How he approaches a problem, and one thing he could not resolve | He is not overselling | — |
| **5min** | The range and organisation of the work | There is judgment here worth talking to | **"He shows his reasoning, and says what he couldn't work out"** |

**The 5-minute memory is the whole objective**, and there should only be one. If a visitor cannot complete *"the interesting thing about that site was…"*, the homepage has failed regardless of appearance.

---

## 4. What the homepage deliberately excludes

| Excluded | Why |
|---|---|
| Skills / tools list | Metadata. Belongs on About |
| Certifications | Trust Model ranks these low. Never a homepage element |
| Stat counters | Corporate-genericity marker; Het's numbers are small and would invite comparison |
| Project card grid | Rejected as the primary experience; also inverts reasoning-before-artifact |
| Roadmap | Most impressive-looking, least substantiated. Its own page, reached deliberately |
| Photo | Optional, undecided, and not load-bearing |
| Testimonials | None exist |
| "Currently available" banner | Dates badly; belongs on About |
| Newsletter signup | Promises a cadence that is unverified |

**The roadmap exclusion is deliberate and worth restating.** 219 documented concepts against zero built repositories is the site's most dangerous asset. On the homepage it would read as planning substituting for doing.

## 5. Empty-state behaviour

The homepage must work at every content volume.

| Investigations | Position 3 | Position 4 |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Cannot launch. **The site requires at least one** | — |
| **1** | The single investigation, given full space | Omitted entirely — no "more coming soon" |
| **2–4** | Strongest investigation | Short list of the rest |
| **5–15** | Most recent or featured | List of ~5, link to the index |
| **16+** | Featured | List of ~5, link to index; field notes gain their own position |

**Sections disappear rather than showing empty states.** A homepage with three positions is complete; a homepage with five positions and two of them empty is broken. This is the mechanism that lets the site launch honestly at low volume.

---

## 6. Failure modes

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| Reads as a resume | No skills, certifications, or credential sections |
| Reads as a project gallery | No card grid; reasoning precedes artifacts |
| Recruiter cannot orient | Position 1 answers everything in one sentence |
| Practitioner sees nothing of substance | Position 3 shows real reasoning, not a teaser |
| Looks empty at low volume | Positions collapse rather than emptying |
| Weak IA rescued by motion | **Explicitly forbidden.** The sequence must work as a static document |
