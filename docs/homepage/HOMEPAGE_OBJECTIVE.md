# Homepage Objective

**Date:** 2026-08-15 (Phase 5E)

---

## 1. What the homepage is for

The site is a **personal professional portfolio**. It is not a blog, a publication, a newsletter, a documentation site or a journal. The Investigation system is a *different way of presenting portfolio work* — it is not the reason the site exists.

The homepage has one job: **make a security hiring reader understand who Het is and why he is worth talking to, fast enough that they keep reading.**

Everything else — the reasoning, the uncertainty, the revision register — is what makes that portfolio unusual once they do.

---

## 2. The governing constraint

**T9 — the portfolio must read as a portfolio before it reads as a publication.**

This exists because Phase 5D measured a real failure. The rendered investigation page produced:

| | |
|---|---|
| **First impression (5s)** | Editorial publication |
| **After full reading** | Personal working record |

Acceptable for an investigation page. **Fatal as the site's primary identity.** The failure it names is a recruiter thinking *"interesting cybersecurity writer"* rather than *"interesting cybersecurity professional with unusually strong evidence and reasoning."*

---

## 3. Questions the first screen must answer

Without scrolling, at 375px:

1. **Who is Het?**
2. **What does he do?** — the *lane*, nameable, not a mission statement
3. **What security work has he actually done?** — that it is professional, not coursework
4. **Where is the evidence?**

The 5E research round is unambiguous on point 2: a reader who cannot identify the lane in about five seconds leaves. Het's lane is a genuine intersection — **technical security testing and regulated assurance** — and stating it is the single highest-value sentence on the site.

---

## 4. Time-based targets

| Time | The visitor should reach |
|---|---|
| **5 s** | "This isn't another cybersecurity student portfolio." |
| **20–30 s** | "I understand who Het is and what kind of security work he does." |
| **2 min** | "He has actual experience, not just certificates and labs." |
| **5 min** | "He thinks about security problems rather than listing tools." |
| **10 min+** | "I want to open one of these investigations." |
| **Eventually** | "I should talk to this person." |

**Not optimised for:** "cool animation." Optimised for: *"interesting person — let's look further."*

---

## 5. Audiences

**Primary:** security hiring managers · security engineers · security recruiters · technical interviewers · strong engineering organisations.
**Secondary:** security practitioners · collaborators · researchers · academic contacts.

The homepage must serve a recruiter scanning in 20 seconds **and** a practitioner exploring for 10 minutes, without a "recruiter view" toggle or audience segmentation. Layered entry, not branching.

---

## 6. What the homepage must be honest about

The hard limits are unchanged and the homepage is designed around them, not against them:

- **Zero verified built work.** GitHub holds no verified original repositories.
- **One ~7-month internship**, plus confirmed CSCRF/IRDAI audit involvement.
- **No project descriptions yet.**
- **No published writing yet.**

Two consequences follow directly.

**First: professional experience is the strongest verified asset, and the homepage must reflect that.** `PROJECT_CONSTRAINTS.md` says so plainly — *"the professional work, not the roadmap, is the credible core."* Phase 3's IA gave it two or three sentences inside another position. That inverts the evidence and is revised (P5EV-004).

**Second: the homepage must be credible at launch volume.** No fake metrics, no empty grids, no "coming soon", no placeholder projects, no quantity-as-credibility. **Sections disappear rather than emptying.**

---

## 7. The line the homepage must not cross

Het's advantage is **access-derived, not skill-derived**. He was placed inside real regulated engagements at a stage when almost nobody is. That entitles him to claim *having seen how regulated assurance is actually conducted, and having formed judgment from it.* It does not entitle him to claim expertise, seniority or authority.

Role precision is mandatory: **"I worked on" and "I assisted with" are accurate; "I conducted" and "I led" are not.**

Overclaiming converts the strongest asset into the biggest liability with exactly the reader Het needs.

---

## 8. Success criteria

The homepage succeeds if:

1. The dominant interpretation is **portfolio**, not publication/blog/consultancy/documentation.
2. A recruiter can answer §3's four questions in 20 seconds.
3. A visitor who reads only the homepage has still seen **one real piece of reasoning and one real admitted limit** — the surviving core of the Phase 4 H1 decision.
4. It is credible at 3 investigations and still works at 25 without redesign.
5. Replacing "Het Patel" with a generic name makes it visibly weaker — because the differentiation is content, not decoration.
6. Nothing on it is fabricated.
