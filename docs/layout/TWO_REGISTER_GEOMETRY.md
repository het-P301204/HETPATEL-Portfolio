# Two-Register Geometry — Model Stress Test

**Date:** 2026-08-14 (Phase 5B) · **The most important document in Phase 5B.**

---

## 1. The challenge, answered directly

> *Has the distributed apparatus become a conventional editorial masthead with metadata?*

**Largely yes — and that must be conceded before anything else.**

Phase 5A specified the masthead as carrying **status, date, domain, origin**. Set beneath a title in a secondary type register, that is structurally indistinguishable from a conventional article byline. Every technical blog has one: title, date, author, tags.

**What is actually unconventional about it is content, not geometry:**

| Field | Conventional? |
|---|---|
| Date | Entirely conventional |
| Domain | Conventional — this is tags |
| Status | Unusual — most portfolios have no state model |
| **`origin`** (professional / self-initiated) | **Genuinely unusual** |
| **`unresolved`** as a permitted status | **Genuinely unusual** |

**So the "two-register system" as specified in 5A oversells itself.** At launch it is a well-set article with an unusually honest byline. The inline insets — the part that would create a real second register — were specified as *occasional*, and are the element most likely to be empty.

This is a real finding, and defending Model C as originally written would be wrong.

---

## 2. What the stress test revealed

The distinctive thing about the apparatus is **not the metadata at the head. It is the revision record.**

An investigation that carries, at its foot:

```
2026-03-14   published
2026-06-02   revised — the conclusion about ordering was wrong; corrected above
2026-09-19   revised — added the case I had missed
```

…is doing something no portfolio in the research corpus does. It shows **thinking that changed**, dated. That is *"reasoning, shown"* extended across time rather than across a page.

**And it is spatially distinctive**, because a document with a growing ledger at its foot does not look like an article. It looks like a record that is still being kept.

## 4. Recommendation: Model C′ — distributed apparatus with an accreting foot register

**Three positions, each with a different temporal character:**

| Position | Contains | Present at launch? | Grows? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head** | status · date · domain · origin | **Always** — never empty | No |
| **Inline** | asides at the point of relevance | Rarely | Slightly |
| **Foot** | dated revision record | **Usually empty** | **Yes — this is the point** |

**Why this is a genuine improvement on 5A's Model C, not a rescue:**

1. **It concedes the head is a byline** and stops claiming spatial novelty for it.
2. **It relocates the distinctiveness to where it actually lives** — the temporal record.
3. **The foot register is honestly empty at launch** and renders as nothing (VP-7). Nothing is advertised.
4. **It gets stronger with age rather than with volume.** A single investigation revised twice over a year is more distinctive than ten unrevised ones.

---

## 5. The honest caveat

**At launch, this site's spatial distinctiveness is low.** It is a very well-set reading column with an honest byline. The foot register will be empty on every page for months.

That is acceptable **only because the claim has been correctly downgraded**: per the approved genericity position, the system is *diagnostic*, not unique. It reveals content behaviour rather than manufacturing distinction.

**But it sharpens the Phase 4 exit condition.** Previously: *if the apparatus is inert at six months, collapse to editorial.* Now more precisely:

> **If no investigation has acquired a revision entry within twelve months, the foot register should be removed and the site should be acknowledged as a well-made editorial reading site — which is a good outcome, just not a distinctive one.**

---

## 6. Geometry

**All three positions share the reading column's left edge.** No indentation, no offset, no hanging.

| Position | Relationship |
|---|---|
| Head | Immediately after the title. Closer to the title than to the prose — it belongs to the heading |
| Inline | In the flow, at the point of relevance. More space above than below |
| Foot | After all prose and artifacts. Separated by MAJOR space, not a rule |

**Width:** head and foot span the full reading measure. Insets may be marginally narrower on the right only.

**Density:** head is dense (compact label/value pairs). Foot is a dated list, also dense. **Density is the register signal**, alongside type.

**Responsive:** no transformation. Identical structure at every width.
