# About Layout

**Date:** 2026-08-14 (Phase 5B)

**Purpose:** the one page that answers *who is this* directly. It carries what the investigations cannot — employment history, education, credentials, contact.

---

## Sequence and space allocation

```
Identity                      2–3 sentences        MOST SPACE
  OPEN
What I work on                2–3 paragraphs       SUBSTANTIAL
  OPEN
Professional experience       role, org, period,   MODERATE
                              method-level
  OPEN
Education                     one entry            TIGHT
  NORMAL
Certifications                a list               TIGHT
  MAJOR
Contact                       email · GitHub       TIGHT
```

**Space is allocated inversely to convention.** Most portfolios give credentials the most room and identity the least. Here the first two blocks — who he is and what he works on, in his own voice — occupy roughly two-thirds of the page.

**Reason:** credentials rank low in the trust model. The About page's job is to make the *person* legible; the facts are confirmation, not argument.

---

## Professional experience

Prose, not a résumé block.

- Role, organisation, period stated plainly.
- Responsibilities at **method level** — what kind of work, not what was found.
- **Role precision required:** "I worked on", "I assisted with". Never "I led" or "I conducted" unless verified.
- CSCRF and IRDAI may be named as frameworks worked on. **No client, no findings, no metrics, no correlatable dates.**
- **Nothing draws attention to what is withheld.** Omission is silent.

**Not a timeline graphic.** A dated paragraph is more precise and carries no implication of a longer history than exists.

---

## Education and certifications

**Compact, factual, low on the page.**

- Education: one entry — institution, degree, period.
- Certifications: a plain list, once. **No counting** ("16 Credly certifications" as a headline is banned), no logos, no badges, no grid.
- CompTIA Security+ shown as in progress without ceremony.

**TIGHT spacing throughout** — this is reference material, and generous spacing here would give it a prominence the trust model denies it.

---

## Portrait

**If used: small, here, and nowhere else.** Optional; the page is complete without it.

Placement beside the identity block or after it. **Never at the top as a hero.** Never on the homepage.

---

## Contact

Email as a `mailto:` text link. GitHub as a text link. Nothing else.

**No form** — adds abuse surface for no benefit at this scale. **No "available for work" banner** — dates badly. **No call-to-action block.**

---

## What About does not contain

| Absent | Why |
|---|---|
| Skills list / tech stack | Metadata masquerading as content. Tools appear inside method |
| Certification wall or counts | Trust model ranks these low |
| Timeline graphic | A dated paragraph is more precise |
| Personal philosophy essay | The investigations demonstrate it; asserting it is weaker |
| Long autobiography | Two or three paragraphs is the ceiling |
| Downloadable CV as the main event | May exist as a plain link, low |
| The roadmap | Its own page, reached from here, never promoted |
| Testimonials | None exist |

---

## Geometry

- Reading measure slightly tighter than investigations (~66ch) — reference material, read once.
- Same single left edge.
- No containers. Certifications are a list, not a grid.
- Fits comfortably in 1.5–2 screens at desktop. **If it exceeds three, it has become a résumé dump.**
