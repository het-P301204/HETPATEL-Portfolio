# Layout Failure Test

**Date:** 2026-08-14 (Phase 5B) · Twelve attempts to break the recommended system.

---

## F1 — Apparatus becomes decorative

**Cause:** the head carries derived fields (read time, word count, tag counts) added to make it look substantial.
**Mitigation:** four fields only, all editorially real. **No derived metadata, ever.**
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes — this is the concept.

## F2 — Apparatus becomes conventional metadata

**Cause:** it already largely is. At launch the head is a byline.
**Mitigation:** conceded openly in `TWO_REGISTER_GEOMETRY.md` §1. Distinctiveness relocated to `origin`, `unresolved`, and the **foot register** — which is temporal, not spatial.
**Preserves concept?** ⚠️ **Partly.** The "two-register system" framing is weaker than Phase 4/5A claimed. The honest version is *a document with an accreting record*. **This is the most significant finding of Phase 5B.**

## F3 — Too editorial

**Cause:** centred serif column, generous space, no chrome.
**Mitigation:** dense factual apparatus, mono literals, precise language, `origin`/`status` visible on every item. **The apparatus is the counterweight** — a magazine does not tell you whether a piece is unresolved.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes, but it is the closest resemblance and must be watched in 5C.

## F4 — Too academic

**Cause:** serif, long-form, careful hedging.
**Mitigation:** first-person voice; **no citations, no abstract, no footnote apparatus, no references section.** Serif selection in 5C must avoid literary/scholarly faces.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes.

## F5 — Too corporate

**Cause:** structured metadata on every item.
**Mitigation:** no badges, no cards, no logos, no counters, first-person prose, visible uncertainty. **The word `unresolved` is itself anti-corporate** — no consultancy publishes that.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes. Lowest-risk failure.

## F6 — Too sparse

**Cause:** near-zero imagery, motion, containers; short content.
**Mitigation:** pages end where content ends; density where honest (tight index, dense apparatus); launch at three investigations.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes, **conditional on 5C craft.** With nothing to hide behind, mediocre type reads as unfinished rather than restrained.

## F7 — Impressive but hard to read

**Cause:** unlikely here — the layout is optimised for reading. Possible if the measure runs wide or the rhythm goes flat.
**Mitigation:** measure capped at 68ch and validated at three widths in 5D; OPEN:NORMAL ≥ 2.2:1.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes.

## F8 — Mobile destroys the concept

**Cause:** would apply to a side-column model.
**Mitigation:** **structurally impossible in Model C′** — mobile *is* the layout. Nothing reflows, reorders, or hides.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes. Eliminated by construction.

## F9 — Short investigations look empty

**Cause:** 150 words in a wide frame.
**Mitigation:** page terminates cleanly; head apparatus gives factual substance; no minimum heights; genuinely short work goes in **notes** instead.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes. **Residual risk accepted** — a short investigation *is* small, and the design should not disguise that.

## F10 — Long investigations become monotonous

**Cause:** 2,500 words, one column, no rules, few headings.
**Mitigation:** internal L2 headings permitted at length; inline insets break the texture; paragraph-length variation required; possibly one figure.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes — headings are permitted *within* movements, never *as* movement labels.

## F11 — Layout depends on having many projects

**Cause:** index, filters, related work all scale with volume.
**Mitigation:** index does not exist at n=1; filters appear at 10 and 15; related work renders nothing when absent; homepage preview omitted below three.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes. Tested across five volumes.

## F12 — Looks like Tufte CSS

**Cause:** the original two-register idea is margin-note-shaped.
**Mitigation:** **no margin notes exist.** No side region at any breakpoint, no hanging elements, no offset alignment — everything shares one left edge. Model B was rejected specifically on this constraint.
**Preserves concept?** ✅ Yes. **The strongest structural guarantee in the system**, and it is what made Model C′ necessary rather than merely convenient.

## Which failures actually threaten the concept

| Rank | Failure | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **F2 — conventional metadata** | **High** | **Conceded and reframed.** The concept is weaker than claimed |
| 2 | F6 — too sparse | Medium-high | Depends entirely on 5C craft |
| 3 | F3 — too editorial | Medium | Watch in 5C |
| 4 | F9 — short investigations thin | Medium | Residual, accepted |
| 5 | F10 — long monotony | Low-medium | Mitigated |
| 6–12 | Remainder | Low | Structurally handled |

**F2 is the finding of this phase.** Everything else is manageable; F2 required changing what the system claims about itself.
