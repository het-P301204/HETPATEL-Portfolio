## Recommendation: **F — Distributed-document composition**

A single constrained reading column (inheriting everything good about C) in which **apparatus appears at three defined positions rather than one**: the head, occasionally inline, and — the significant addition — **an accreting register at the foot**.

**Why F over C:** constrained-reading composition is the safest and most readable option, and it is what the site would be if the apparatus were only a masthead. F is C plus a temporal dimension. See `TWO_REGISTER_GEOMETRY.md` for why that addition is what rescues the concept.

**Why not B or E:** both reintroduce a side region, which brings back the empty-gutter risk that Phase 5A eliminated, and both push toward the Tufte resemblance that is explicitly prohibited.

**Why not D:** full-width composition fails the primary activity. This is a reading site.

---

## The character in one line

> **One column, one left edge, three apparatus positions, no containers.**

---

## What the layout must communicate without decoration

| Quality | Spatial expression |
|---|---|
| **Deliberate** | Every element aligns to one left edge. Nothing is placed for visual interest |
| **Unhurried** | Generous vertical space around prose; the column does not grow to fill the viewport |
| **Precise** | Apparatus is dense and exactly aligned; metadata never approximated |
| **Candid** | The uncertainty movement occupies the same space as every other movement — no more, no less |
| **Spare** | Two containers site-wide; one rule; no cards |
| **Grounded** | Dates visible at head and foot; the document is always situated in time |

---

## The governing constraint

**Layout may not compensate for content.** No minimum heights, no padding to fill, no sections that exist to occupy space. A short page is short.

This is what makes the system honest at one investigation and unchanged at fifty.
