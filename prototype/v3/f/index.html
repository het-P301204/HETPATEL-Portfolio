On this record

Each entry is a question I worked through, written with what I found and what I could not determine. The second part is not modesty. An investigation that ends "I could not establish which of these three explanations was responsible" is more useful than one that quietly picks the likeliest, because it tells you where the evidence stopped.

When I get something wrong I say so on the page, with a date, rather than editing it away. Where a conclusion has changed, the entry records what it used to be and why it changed.

The professional work behind this — the CSCRF and IRDAI engagements — is not published here and will not be. I assisted on it; I did not scope, own or lead it, and I am not an auditor. Seven months is exposure, not expertise.
