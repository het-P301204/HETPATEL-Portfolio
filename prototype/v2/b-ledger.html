A finding and a control are not the same object.

I test web applications for vulnerabilities, and I work on the compliance side of the same problem. A tester asks whether something is exploitable. An audit asks whether the organisation can demonstrate that it manages the risk. Those are different questions, satisfied by different evidence — and the translation between them is where I have learned the most.
