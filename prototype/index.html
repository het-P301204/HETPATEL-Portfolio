Why an allow-list kept failing open

Status
closed
Origin
self-initiated

A test environment I had built for something else kept accepting requests that its allow-list should have rejected. The rule read correctly. I wanted to know where the gap between the declared rule and the observed behaviour was coming from, because a rule that is right on paper and wrong in practice is a more interesting failure than a rule that is simply wrong.

My first instinct was to read the configuration, and I deliberately did not. Reading the rule first tends to make you see what you expect to see. So I started from the other end: I captured what actually reached the service and worked backwards toward the rule. The matcher was being handed the pre-normalisation path.

What I could not determine was whether the ordering was deliberate. There is a plausible performance argument for normalising once, late, rather than on every candidate comparison — and if that was the reasoning, the failure is a consequence of a defensible decision rather than an oversight. I have left it open rather than guessing, because the two call for different fixes.
